## Clinical Summary: Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF)

- **Primary Clinical Focus:**
    Demystifying Childhood Tic Disorders and Tourette Syndrome (TS)—distinguishing common Provisional/Transient Tic Disorder (which affects up to 15–20% of elementary-school children and fades within  =2 motor tics + >=1 vocal tic lasting >1 year with onset before age 18).

- **Evidence-Based Protocol & Targets:**
    First reassure parents about the #1 media myth: involuntary swearing (coprolalia) occurs in LESS than 10% of people with Tourette Syndrome—most vocal tics are simple throat-clearing, sniffing, grunting, or coughing (often mistaken for allergies or asthma!); NEVER tell a child to 'stop doing that' or draw constant attention to a new tic (parental vigilance increases anxiety and amplifies the basal ganglia premonitory itch); utilize Comprehensive Behavioral Intervention for Tics (CBIT / Habit Reversal Training) as the American Academy of Neurology (AAN) #1 first-line treatment; and screen for the 'Tourette Triad' (60% have co-occurring ADHD and 50% have OCD, which usually cause far more school impairment than the tics themselves!).

- **When to Escalate to OB-GYN or Emergency Care:**
    Seek prompt pediatric evaluation if severe neck-whipping tics risk cervical spine injury, if tics emerge explosively overnight in a pre-pubertal child following a Group A Strep infection accompanied by acute OCD, bedwetting, and handwriting deterioration (evaluate for PANDAS/PANS), or if tics are paired with loss of consciousness or unresponsive staring (rule out absence/myoclonic seizures via EEG).

         Overnight 'Lightning-Bolt' Explosion of Tics + Severe OCD + Strep Throat / Fever History
         Pregnant or Postpartum Mother With Tourette Syndrome (Medication &amp; Delivery Planning)

       2. Co-Occurring Symptoms or School Impact

         Child Barely Notices the Tics (Mostly Happens When Tired, Excited, or Watching Screens)
         Also Struggling With Focus/Hyperactivity (ADHD), Evening Rituals (OCD), or Social Teasing
         Tics Are Causing Neck/Joint Pain, Bullied at School, or Interfering With Writing

### Amber Zone: Prompt OB-GYN or Specialist Workup

        - Taking your child to 4 allergy/asthma specialists for 'constant sniffing and throat clearing' that never happens while the child is asleep (classic simple phonic tic!).

        - Tics that last >12 months, cause sore neck/shoulder muscles, or are accompanied by ADHD inattention or bedtime OCD rituals.

        - Noticing a spike in tics right after starting a high-dose stimulant for ADHD (note: modern AAN guidelines confirm most kids with Tourette's + ADHD *can* take stimulants safely, or pair/switch to Guanfacine ER!).

         TIER 3 • IMMEDIATE EMERGENCY / L&amp;D TRIAGE

### Red Zone: Urgent Emergency Medical Intervention

        - Violent head-jerking or neck-snapping motor tics that cause neck pain, tingling in the arms, or risk cervical radiculopathy.

        - Explosive, overnight onset of severe tics, food refusal, bedwetting, and panic in a child who recently had strep throat (evaluate for PANDAS).

        - Episodes where the child's eyes roll back and they are completely unresponsive to their name or touch for 5–15 seconds (rule out absence seizures).
