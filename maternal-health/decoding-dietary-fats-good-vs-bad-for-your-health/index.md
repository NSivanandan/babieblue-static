### Amber Zone: Prompt OB-GYN or Specialist Workup

        - Avoiding all seafood out of fear of mercury without taking a DHA/algal supplement (studies show completely skipping fish without supplementing deprives the fetal brain of DHA and iodine).

        - Relying only on flaxseed oil or chia seeds for pregnancy omega-3s (less than 5% converts into DHA!).

        - Getting a routine cholesterol blood test during pregnancy or while breastfeeding and panicking because your LDL/triglycerides are elevated (pregnancy and lactation naturally raise cholesterol by 40–60% to make hormones and breastmilk—wait until 3 months after weaning for an accurate baseline!).

         TIER 3 • IMMEDIATE EMERGENCY / L&amp;D TRIAGE

### Red Zone: Urgent Emergency Medical Intervention

        - Eating high-mercury predator fish (Swordfish, Shark, King Mackerel, Tilefish, Bigeye Tuna) during pregnancy or lactation.

        - Developing intense, rash-free itching on the palms of your hands and soles of your feet in the 2nd or 3rd trimester (Intrahepatic Cholestasis of Pregnancy—requires urgent Serum Bile Acid lab test).

        - Severe, boring pain in the upper middle abdomen radiating straight through to your back with vomiting (acute gallstone pancreatitis).
