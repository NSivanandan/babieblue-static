## Clinical Summary: Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF)

- **Primary Clinical Focus:**
    Executing a medically safe 'babymoon' or family trip using ACOG Committee Opinion #746 (Air Travel During Pregnancy) and CDC Yellow Book Perinatal Travel Medicine protocols.

- **Evidence-Based Protocol & Targets:**
    Schedule travel during the Golden Second-Trimester Window (Weeks 14 to 28, when morning sickness has faded and preterm labor risk is lowest); observe airline gestational cutoffs (36w0d for domestic flights; 28–35w for international flights; 24w0d for all cruise lines!); prevent in-flight Deep Vein Thrombosis (pregnancy + cabin immobilization increases VTE risk 5x–10x) by wearing 15–30 mmHg graduated compression socks, booking an aisle seat to walk every 60 minutes, and drinking 8 oz water per flight hour; wear your seatbelt LOW across your hipbones under the belly at all times; and never travel to active Zika or Malaria zones while pregnant.

- **When to Escalate to OB-GYN or Emergency Care:**
    Before leaving home, locate the nearest hospital with a Level III/IV NICU and Labor & Delivery unit at your destination, carry a printed/PDF copy of your prenatal chart, and seek immediate local hospital care for vaginal bleeding, leaking fluid, >=6 contractions/hour, unilateral calf swelling, or severe headache/vision changes.

### Amber Zone: Prompt OB-GYN or Specialist Workup

        - Booking a cruise without realizing that all cruise lines deny boarding if you hit 24 weeks and 0 days at any point during the trip!

        - Reaching for Pepto-Bismol or Kaopectate for vacation upset stomach (never use bismuth subsalicylate in pregnancy—use Tums, Pepcid/Famotidine, or Zofran/Unisom).

        - Eating unpasteurized local soft cheeses, raw oysters/ceviche, cold buffet deli meats, or tap-water ice cubes in developing regions.

         TIER 3 • IMMEDIATE EMERGENCY / L&amp;D TRIAGE

### Red Zone: Urgent Emergency Medical Intervention

        - Traveling to an active Zika outbreak area (causes severe fetal microcephaly) or Chloroquine-resistant Malaria zone while pregnant.

        - Scuba diving at any depth while pregnant (fetal circulation bypasses the lungs via the foramen ovale/ductus arteriosus, so nitrogen microbubbles go straight to the fetal brain!).

        - Developing unilateral calf swelling/pain during or up to 2 weeks after a long flight or road trip (urgent ultrasound to rule out DVT).
