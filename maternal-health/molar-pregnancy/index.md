## Clinical Summary: Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF)

- **Primary Clinical Focus:**
    Differentiating Complete Hydatidiform Mole (46,XX diploid androgenetic—empty ovum fertilized by duplicated sperm) from Partial Hydatidiform Mole (69,XXX/XXY diandric triploid) and executing mandatory post-evacuation quantitative beta-hCG surveillance to prevent or cure Gestational Trophoblastic Neoplasia (GTN).

- **Evidence-Based Protocol & Targets:**
    Treat diagnosed molar pregnancy via ultrasound-guided suction dilation and curettage (D&C), administer RhoGAM if Rh-negative, and track serum quantitative beta-hCG every 1–2 weeks until 3 consecutive normal ( 98% cure rate) if post-evacuation beta-hCG plateaus across 4 readings over 3 weeks or rises >=10% across 3 readings over 2 weeks.
