## Clinical Summary: Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF)

At 2 months old , your baby's optimal homeostatic sleep-pressure wake window is 1.15 to 1.9 hours between sleep periods, supporting 4–5 naps/day and 14.0–16.5 hrs of total 24-hour sleep. Use the shorter end of the window ( 1.15 hrs ) before the first morning nap and the longest window ( 1.9 hrs ) prior to bedtime to build sufficient adenosine sleep drive without triggering an overtired cortisol surge.

## 3-Tier Pediatric Sleep & Airway Safety Triage (2-Month-Old)

         TIER 1 &bull; PHYSIOLOGICAL NORM

### Expected Developmental Sleep Pattern

        - Wake windows of 1.15–1.9 hours with 4–5 naps/day and short 30–45 minute naps during developmental regressions.

        - Brief 1–2 minute vocalizations between 45-minute NREM-REM sleep cycles while connecting naps.

         TIER 2 &bull; SAME-WEEK MD CONSULT

### Pediatrician Evaluation Recommended

        - Hourly night wakings with extreme restlessness, leg kicking, or pica after 6 months (request Serum Ferritin check; target >=50 ng/mL).

        - Persistent arches/crying when laid flat after feeds or poor weight velocity.

         TIER 3 &bull; IMMEDIATE ER / L&amp;D TRIAGE

### Urgent Airway / Medical Evaluation

        - Habitual loud snoring, mouth breathing, witnessed apneic pauses, or gasping during sleep.

        - Cyanosis (blue/dusky lips), stridor at rest, or lethargy / difficulty waking for feeds.

## Frequently Asked Clinical Questions

                How long should a 2-month-old stay awake between naps?
A 2-month-old baby thrives on wake windows of 1.15 to 1.9 hours. Follow sleepy cues (red eyebrows, zoning out, decreased activity) 15 minutes before the end of the window.
    How many naps does a 2-month-old need?
At 2 months, most infants require 4–5 naps/day, with total 24-hour sleep averaging 14.0–16.5 hrs.
