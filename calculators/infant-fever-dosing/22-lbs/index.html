Infant Tylenol & Motrin Fever Dosing for a 22 lb (9.98 kg) Baby

Exact AAP weight-calibrated liquid Acetaminophen (3.9 mL) and Ibuprofen dosing chart, oral syringe measurement guide, and fever escalation protocol for a 22-pound infant.

Clinical Summary: Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF)

For a 22 lb (9.98 kg) baby, the standard AAP weight-calibrated Acetaminophen (Tylenol) 160 mg/5 mL liquid dose is 3.9 mL (124.8 mg) every 4 to 6 hours as needed (never exceed 5 doses in 24 hours). If your baby is at least 6 months old, the Ibuprofen (Motrin/Advil) dose is 4.99 mL of 100 mg/5 mL oral suspension OR 2.5 mL of 50 mg/1.25 mL concentrated infant drops every 6 to 8 hours.

Exact Syringe Measurement Protocol for 22 lbs (9.98 kg)

Pediatric antipyretic dosing is strictly governed by body weight in kilograms (9.98 kg) rather than chronological age. Always measure liquid medication using a calibrated oral dosing syringe (marked in milliliters, mL) rather than a household kitchen spoon.

3-Tier Pediatric Fever Triage Protocol (22 lb Infant)

TIER 1 • PHYSIOLOGICAL NORM

Home Comfort & Oral Hydration

  • Infant is ≥3 months old, alert, taking breastmilk/formula, and playful when temperature drops after 3.9 mL acetaminophen.
  • Dress in a single breathable layer; avoid cold baths or alcohol rubs which trigger shivering vasoconstriction.
TIER 2 • SAME-WEEK MD CONSULT

Same-Day Pediatrician Evaluation

  • Infant is 2 to 3 months old with any fever ≥100.4°F (call pediatrician before or immediately after dosing).
  • Fever persists >72 hours, reaches ≥104.0°F repeatedly, or is accompanied by ear tugging or painful urination.
TIER 3 • IMMEDIATE ER / L&D TRIAGE

Immediate Emergency Department (911 / ER)

  • Infant is under 60 days old with a rectal temperature ≥100.4°F (38.0°C) — do not mask fever prior to ER exam.
  • Inconsolable high-pitched crying, bulging fontanelle, purple non-blanching rash, grunting, or febrile seizure.

Frequently Asked Clinical Questions

How many mL of infant Tylenol (160 mg/5 mL) can a 22 lb baby take?

A 22 lb (9.98 kg) baby takes 3.9 mL (124.8 mg) of 160 mg/5 mL liquid acetaminophen every 4 to 6 hours as needed, up to a maximum of 5 doses in 24 hours. If your infant is under 12 weeks old, always call your pediatrician before giving Tylenol.

Can a 22 lb baby take Motrin (Ibuprofen)?

Only if your 22 lb baby is at least 6 months old. For infants 6 months and older weighing 22 lbs (9.98 kg), the dose is 4.99 mL of 100 mg/5 mL Children's Ibuprofen or 2.5 mL of 50 mg/1.25 mL Infant Drops every 6 to 8 hours.
