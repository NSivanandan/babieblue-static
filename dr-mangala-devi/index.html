Dr. Mangala Devi K.R., MBBS, MS (OBG), MBA

Secretary (IFS Karnataka) • Immediate Past President (BSOG) • Consultant at Manipal Hospitals (MHEL) • Education: CREST, Singapore

Dr. Mangala Devi K.R. is a Senior Medical Reviewer at Babie Blue, a Consultant in Obstetrics & Gynaecology at Manipal Hospitals (MHEL), Bengaluru, and Clinical Director of Smile Baby IVF. With over 24 years of clinical experience in high-risk obstetrics, reproductive endocrinology, and assisted reproductive technology (ART), she reviews Babie Blue's obstetric, fertility, and maternal health protocols.
