Recurring Revenue Building Blocks - How I Turn This Static Site Into a High-AdSense Revenue Machine

Written by: Sivanandan N | Practical AI Newsletter founder
✓ Strategy-First • Data-Backed • Actionable
2026-10-23 Category: Blog

Here is what I see when I review Babie Blue's static website deployment at scale. Every page has the same ad setup: three AdSense slots per page — top banner, in-article mid-article, bottom-post, and a full-width sidebar rectangle. That is already 4x impressions per page. But most site owners don't go deeper. Here is exactly how I would turn this into a revenue multiplier engine.

The Critical Blocker: Ads.txt Placeholder

The very first thing I noticed is the ads.txt file. It contains ca-pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX — a placeholder. Google AdSense will not serve any ads on this site until this is replaced with your real publisher ID.

Fix: Go to your Google AdSense dashboard → Revenue → Health check → Confirm properties → Copy your Publisher ID from the top of the page → Paste it into /ads.txt.

Slot Analysis: What Each Ad Spot Actually Produces

Based on industry benchmarks for medical/parenting niches (CPMs typically $8-25), here is how each slot breaks down:

Slot Format Estimated CPM Why It Works Here
Top Banner (home & all pages) Leaderboard / Auto-ads $10-18 High impressions, auto-ads covers it well
In-Article Mid-Post Fluid responsive $6-12 Highest user attention window
Bottom Post Ad Fluid responsive $5-9 Natural reading-end placement
Sidebar (half-page) Medium rectangle $7-11 Sticky, high viewability

With Auto-ads enabled, Google dynamically places ads. With manual slots defined, you control the exact placement. For maximum revenue, I would use manual slots for top banner and sidebar, and keep Auto-ads for in-article. This hybrid strategy historically lifts eCPM by 20-40% because Auto-ads finds the best performing positions autonomously.

Why This Site Can Hit $X Per Month

At 159 pages with an average of 3 ad slots each, that is roughly 477 ad impressions per full-site crawl. With 5,000 monthly unique visitors averaging 2.5 pages per session, that is approximately 12,500 ad page views. At a blended $15 RPM (realistic for US/UK traffic on medical content), that equals roughly $187/month. But the real revenue comes from improving traffic quality and click-through.

The Revenue Stack

Here is the full revenue stack I would deploy on Babie Blue:

  1. AdSense ($150-400/month realistic ceiling)
  2. Affiliate: Baby formula brands / healthcare products
  3. Affiliate: Mental wellness apps
  4. Consulting / sponsorships
