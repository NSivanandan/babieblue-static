About Babie Blue

Evidence-Based Maternal, Reproductive & Pediatric Health Publishing

Babie Blue (BabieBlue.com) is an independent healthcare publication providing clinical guides, interactive calculators, and practical care protocols across fertility, pregnancy, postpartum recovery, and early childhood development.

Every medical article and interactive tool published on Babie Blue is developed by experienced healthcare writers and reviewed by board-certified Obstetricians, Gynaecologists, and Reproductive Medicine specialists to ensure alignment with current clinical guidelines (ACOG, AAP, FOGSI, IFS, and WHO).

Editorial Governance

Clinical Verification & Publishing Standards

1. Evidence-Based Sourcing

All clinical recommendations, lab reference intervals, and pediatric dosing tables are sourced from peer-reviewed medical societies including ACOG, AAP, FOGSI, IFS, and NASPGHAN.

2. Physician Peer Review

Medical guides and calculator formulas are reviewed by Dr. Mangala Devi K.R. and Dr. Anagha Nawal for clinical accuracy and patient safety.

3. Editorial Independence

Clinical reviews operate independently of commercial sponsorships. Product evaluations rely on objective safety certifications and third-party toxicology data. Contact our team.
