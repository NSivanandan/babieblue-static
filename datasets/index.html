Babie Blue Open Clinical Datasets, REST API & Embeddable Medical Widgets

Free, machine-readable obstetric, pediatric, and maternal endocrinology datasets (`CSV` & `JSON`), deterministic clinical REST endpoints, and zero-dependency embeddable calculators for OB-GYN clinics, doulas, researchers, and AI agents.

Curated by: Madhuri B (Lead Healthcare Content Specialist) • Medically Verified by: Dr. Mangala Devi K.R., MS (OBG) & Dr. Anagha Nawal, MD, MCE, FRM

1. Open-Access Clinical Datasets (CSV & JSON Downloads)

All datasets below are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC-BY-4.0). Researchers, health journalists, academic libraries, and AI developers may freely ingest and republish these tables with canonical attribution to https://babieblue.com/.

Dataset 01 • Pediatrics & Circadian Science

Pediatric Wake Windows & Nap Consolidation Matrix (0–24 Months)

Age-stratified wake window minimums/maximums, nap counts, total 24-hour sleep targets, and AAP safe sleep milestones across 14 developmental stages.

Download CSV View JSON Clinical Guide →
Dataset 02 • Pediatric Pharmacology

AAP Weight-Based Infant Antipyretic Dosing Matrix (6–36 lbs)

Exact weight-calibrated mL and mg dosing for Acetaminophen (160 mg / 5 mL) and Ibuprofen (100 mg / 5 mL & 50 mg / 1.25 mL concentrated drops).

Download CSV View JSON Clinical Guide →
Dataset 03 • Reproductive Endocrinology

Beta-hCG Days Past Ovulation (8–26 DPO) Singleton vs. Twin Norms

Quantitative serum beta-hCG median mIU/mL benchmarks, 10th–90th percentile boundaries, twin gestation medians, and 48-hour doubling velocity rules.

Download CSV View JSON hCG Calculator →
Dataset 04 • Fourth-Trimester Medicine

Postpartum & Maternal Functional Biomarker Reference Targets

Standard laboratory cutoffs vs. optimal functional postpartum recovery targets for Serum Ferritin (≥50 ng/mL), TSH, 25-OH Vitamin D, Hemoglobin, and Vitamin B12.

Download CSV View JSON Lab Panel Guide →
