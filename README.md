# BabieBlue.com

Official static web publication for **[BabieBlue.com](https://babieblue.com)**.

## About BabieBlue

**BabieBlue.com** is an evidence-based maternal health, pregnancy, and pediatric wellness publication dedicated to empowering expecting mothers, new parents, and caregivers with clear, clinically grounded guidance.

## Core Content Areas

- **Maternal Health & Pregnancy Care**: Antenatal development, trimester milestones, safe exercises, nutrition tips, and postpartum recovery.
- **Newborn & Infant Care**: Feeding schedules, bottle refusal fixes, gentle burping techniques, and safe sleep routines.
- **Pediatric Utilities & Calculators**: Interactive due date calculators, ovulation trackers, and maternal health tools.

## Editorial & Clinical Standards

Content published on BabieBlue.com is created by specialized healthcare writers and audited against clinical guidelines established by leading pediatric and obstetric authorities (AAP, ACOG, and WHO).
