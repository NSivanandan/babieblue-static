Affiliate & Advertising Disclosure

Last Updated: November 2026

Babie Blue (https://babieblue.com/) maintains strict clinical and editorial independence. Our obstetric protocols, pediatric dosing matrices, safety evaluations, and medical calculators are authored and reviewed by our healthcare editorial team and board-certified physicians (Dr. Mangala Devi K.R., MBBS, MS (OBG), MBA and Dr. Anagha Nawal, MBBS, DGO, MD, MCE, FRM) without commercial influence.

How We Fund Open-Access Clinical Publishing (Google AdSense)

To keep our clinical calculators, open CSV/JSON medical datasets, and peer-reviewed guides 100% free and accessible without paywalls for all families, Babie Blue is funded through programmatic display advertising served by Google AdSense (Verified Publisher ID: pub-4453876229797223; verified in /ads.txt).

Our Clinical Editorial Firewall & Patient Safety Guarantee

  • Complete Separation of Editorial & Advertising: Advertisers never influence our clinical guidelines, pediatric medication dosing charts, diagnostic matrices, or medical review board conclusions.
  • Zero Health-Input Tracking: Inputs entered into our client-side calculators (such as LMP dates, beta-hCG levels, or infant weight in pounds/kilograms) are processed strictly in your browser memory and are never transmitted to advertising networks.
  • Clear Ad Labeling & Placement Safety: Any programmatic advertisement displayed on Babie Blue is clearly distinguishable from peer-reviewed medical content and is never placed inside emergency red-flag triage boxes or medication syringe outputs.
  • Objective Regulatory Benchmarks: All nursery gear, infant formula, and medical device evaluations rely strictly on objective regulatory standards (FDA, AAP, ACOG, WHO, CPSC 16 CFR 1241, GOTS, and GREENGUARD Gold).

For questions regarding our advertising policy or privacy controls, please review our Privacy Policy or contact our editorial office.
