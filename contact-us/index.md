## Our Clinical Corrections & Verification Protocol

Babie Blue maintains a strict continuous audit cycle across all 185+ articles and interactive calculators. When a reader, clinician, or researcher flags a dosage table, diagnostic reference interval, or US CPT/ICD-10 billing code:

            - Triage Within 24 Hours: Our healthcare editorial lead ( Madhuri B ) logs the citation against primary peer-reviewed literature (ACOG Practice Bulletins, AAP Clinical Practice Guidelines, WHO, CDC, or CMS reimbursement schedules).

            - Physician Sign-Off: Obstetric, fertility, and gynecological updates are reviewed by Dr. Mangala Devi K.R., MS (OBG) and Dr. Anagha Nawal, MD, MCE .

            - Transparent Revision Timestamps: Updated clinical protocols and calculator formulas are deployed immediately with updated review metadata.
