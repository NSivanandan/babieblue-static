Dr. Anagha Nawal is a Senior Medical Reviewer at Babie Blue, a Consultant Obstetrician & Gynaecologist, and a credentialed Clinical Embryologist practicing in Bengaluru. With over 20 years of clinical experience in prenatal care, reproductive genetics, and embryology, she audits Babie Blue's pregnancy, ovulation, hCG, and postpartum recovery guides.
