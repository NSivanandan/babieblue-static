In 2023, there is a strong inclination towards nature-inspired names, reflecting an ever-growing appreciation for the environment and its beauty. Luna, River, and Silas are some of the captivating names that evoke the splendor of the natural world. These names not only sound melodious but also hold deep symbolic meaning.
