Modern parents increasingly favor vowel-initial names—especially 'E' and 'O'—because they begin with immediate vocal-cord resonance rather than a percussive stop, sounding warm, approachable, and international. Whenever a child's first name starts with a vowel (A, E, I, O, U), their 3-letter initials (First-Middle-Last) or 2-letter initials can accidentally spell a word.
