Affiliate & Advertising Disclosure

Last Updated: October 2026

Babie Blue (BabieBlue.com) maintains strict editorial independence. Our clinical protocols, safety evaluations, and medical calculators are developed and reviewed by our healthcare editorial team and board-certified physicians (Dr. Mangala Devi K.R. and Dr. Anagha Nawal) without commercial influence.

How We Fund Clinical Publishing

To keep our clinical calculators and peer-reviewed guides free for all families, Babie Blue participates in select affiliate programs (including Amazon Associates) and displays programmatic advertisements via Google AdSense. If you purchase a product through an outbound retail link, we may earn a small referral commission at no additional cost to you.

Our Editorial Ethics Guarantee

  • We never accept payment or sponsorship in exchange for favorable clinical ratings or product inclusion.
  • All nursery gear, infant formula, and medical device evaluations rely on objective regulatory standards (FDA, CPSC 16 CFR 1241, GOTS, GOLS, and Greenguard Gold).
  • Medical reviewers have final authority over all clinical content and safety warnings.

For questions regarding our publishing standards, please contact our editorial office.
