At Babie Blue ( https://babieblue.com/ ), we recognize that pregnancy, fertility tracking, maternal recovery, and pediatric care involve deeply personal health questions. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you read our clinical protocols or use our interactive calculators.

## 1. Client-Side Processing for Clinical Calculators & Trackers

When you enter clinical values into any Babie Blue interactive calculator—such as your Last Menstrual Period (LMP), IVF embryo transfer date, serial serum Beta-hCG values, infant weight in kilograms/pounds, or hospital bag checklist selections—those calculations are executed exclusively inside your device's web browser using client-side JavaScript.

            - Zero Server Transmission of Health Inputs: Your reproductive dates, lab values, and infant weight inputs are never sent to Babie Blue servers or shared with third-party data brokers.

            - Optional Local Browser Storage: Certain interactive checklists (such as our Interactive Hospital Bag Checklist ) use your browser's local storage ( window.localStorage ) solely so your checked items remain saved on your own device between visits. You can clear this data at any time via our one-click reset buttons or your browser settings.

## 2. Information Collected Automatically (Analytics & Telemetry)

To maintain fast page-load performance and understand which clinical hubs are most useful to families, we collect standard, anonymized web telemetry:

            - Google Analytics 4 (GA4): Collects anonymized page views, device viewport categories, approximate geographic region, and session duration. IP addresses are anonymized by default.

            - Server Log Files: Standard CDN and hosting logs record request timestamps, browser user-agent strings, and referring URLs solely for security, DDoS mitigation, and broken-link auditing.

## 3. Google AdSense, Advertising Cookies & Editorial Firewall

Babie Blue is free to all families and clinicians and is funded exclusively through programmatic display advertising via Google AdSense (Publisher ID: pub-4453876229797223 ). We enforce a strict firewall between advertising and our clinical protocols:

            - Third-Party Advertising Vendors & Google Cookies: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the Google DoubleClick / AdSense cookie) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to BabieBlue.com or other websites on the Internet.

            - Personalized & Non-Personalized Advertising: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our readers based on their visit to BabieBlue.com and/or other sites on the Internet. No clinical calculator inputs (such as LMP dates, beta-hCG values, or infant weights) are ever shared with Google AdSense or any advertising network.

            - How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads: You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings . Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices/ or www.youronlinechoices.eu (for EEA/UK residents).

            - Clinical Editorial Independence: Advertisers have zero influence over our medical guidelines, pediatric dosing tables, or ACOG/AAP/WHO clinical recommendations. Read our full Advertising Policy & Editorial Firewall Disclosure .

## 5. Your Data Privacy Rights (DPDP Act, GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction—including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (India), the General Data Protection Regulation (EU/UK), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (US)—you have the right to:

            - Request confirmation of whether we hold any personal contact information associated with your email address.

            - Request deletion or correction of any correspondence sent to our editorial desk.

            - Opt out of non-essential analytics or advertising cookies through your browser's privacy controls or consent banner.
